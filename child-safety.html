Child safety standards

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Hallmate, published by Alessandro Coron, has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

1. Who can use Hallmate

Hallmate is for adults only. You must be 18 or older, and you can only join a residence with an invite code issued by that residence. Accounts of people under 18 are removed as soon as we learn about them.

2. What is prohibited

  • Any content that sexualises minors, including images, videos, drawings, text and links.
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind.
  • Grooming, soliciting, or attempting to contact a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Sextortion, trafficking, or any other form of sexual exploitation of children.
  • Sharing, requesting, or advertising any of the above, in chats, group chats, posts, listings, comments or profiles.

3. How to report

  • In the app: long-press or open the message, post, listing, comment or profile and choose Report. You can also block the person. Reports go to the residence management and to us.
  • By email: support@hallmate.nl with the subject “Child safety report”. You do not need an account to report.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call the police (112 in the EU).

4. What we do

  • We review child safety reports with priority, as soon as possible and within 24 hours.
  • We remove the content and permanently ban the accounts involved.
  • We preserve the relevant information and report CSAM and child exploitation to the competent authorities, including the Dutch police and, where applicable, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as required by law.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement requests.

5. Child safety contact

The designated point of contact for child safety at Hallmate is Alessandro Coron, reachable at support@hallmate.nl.
